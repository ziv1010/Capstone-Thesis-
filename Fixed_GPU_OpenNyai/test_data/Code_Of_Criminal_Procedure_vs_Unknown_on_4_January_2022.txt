THE HON'BLE SRI JUSTICE CHEEKATI MANAVENDRANATH ROY
 CRIMINAL PETITION NOS.7652 AND 7675 OF 2021
COMMON ORDER:
-
These two criminal petitions under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, are filed
seeking quash of charge sheets filed against the petitioners in C.C.No.239 of 2018 on the file of
learned Additional Junior Civil Judge, Chilakaluripeta, Guntur District, for the offences punishable
under Sections 188, 272, 273, 420 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 (for short "I.P.C.") and under
Sections 58, 59 and 63 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (for short "F.S.S. Act") and in
C.C.No.426 of 2019 on the file of learned Judicial Magistrate of First Class, Allagadda, Kurnool
District, for the offences punishable under Sections 188 and 273 r/w 34 of I.P.C. and under Sections
26 and 30 of F.S.S. Act, respectively.
2. Heard learned counsel for petitioners and the learned Additional Public Prosecutor for the State.
3. These are covered matters in view of the earlier common order passed by this Court in Criminal
Petition No.5421 of 2019 and batch, whereby this Court has quashed the proceedings registered for
the similar offences based on identical facts. Therefore, the petitioners, who are similarly placed, are
also entitled for quash of the proceedings against them.
4. Therefore, in view of the aforesaid common order of this Court and for the reasons stated therein,
these Criminal Petitions are allowed and the aforesaid charge sheets filed against the petitioners, are
hereby quashed.
Miscellaneous Petitions, if any pending, in these Criminal Petitions, shall stand closed.
