2. According to the petitioner, he is a socially active person and Ex- Corporator of Municipal
Corporation. He had filed a PIL raising issue in respect of the construction of '' Treasure Island'' and
''Noise Pollution'', therefore, he may kindly be permitted to file a petition in the nature of PIL.
Looking at his public-spirited antecedent and the nature of the issue raised by him we are deciding
this Public Interest Litigation on merit.
3. According to the petitioner, the Government of India has enacted the law in the name of "the
Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006" ( in short Act of 2006) to check the standard of the food and
to ensure the safety of human life. Under the provision of the aforesaid Act, Manufacturing, storage,
distribution, sale and import of the food articles are liable to be examined by officers and employees
appointed by the appropriate Government from time to time under the Act, 2006. The Act of 2006
provides the establishment of different authorities at Central, State, also local areas for effective
implementation of the provision of the Act.
- : 3 :-
Under the aforesaid Act of 2006, the Food Safety and Standard Authority of India has
been established under Section 4 of the Act which is the parent body and has control
over the overall functioning and enforcement of the Act, 2006. In addition to the
above, there is also a provision of the establishment of State Food Safety Authority in
each State headed by ''Designated Officer'' and '' Food Safety Officer'' at local levels.
Both the officers are having power under the Act of 2006 to take samples of food for
testing, seize any food article, prohibit the sale of unauthorized food and filing of a
criminal case before the competent Court. According to the petitioner, the chapter 8
deals with the procedure for the '' Analysis of Food'' and Chapter 9 provides deals
with the '' Offences and Penalties'' on contravention of the provision of the Act of
2006.

Bhopal, Indore and Ujjain are sanctioned for scientific analysis and to check the standards of
different food articles of the food articles but out of three, only one laboratory is functioning at
Bhopal and the rest at Ujjain and Indore have not even constructed. The petitioner has obtained
information under the Right to Information Act, 2005 that around 50% of samples were not found
up to the mark and failed to satisfy the standards for want of a sufficient number of Food
- : 4 :-
Testing Laboratories in the State of Madhya Pradesh. One laboratory of Bhopal is not
able to handle the entire burden of the State in respect of checking the standards of
seized or suspicious food articles. The seized food articles are required to be checked
within a reasonable time before the expiry of the shelf period. Since there is a delay in
testing, therefore, the offender gets the benefit of acquittal. Hence, present petition
before this Court seeking direction to the respondents to provide tools to the Food
Safety Officers for on the spot analysis of the food article and also direction to the
respondents to make available Food Testing Laboratories at Indore and Ujjain as
functional and provide the Food Testing Laboratories in at every District and
Division of State of Madhya Pradesh to ensure the food safety and standards with
adequate staff.
