1. The Applicant apprehending arrest in connection with the investigation of Crime No. 351 of 2021
of Police Station Walchandnagar, Pune under Section 272, 273, 328 read with Section 34 of IPC and
Section 26(2)(i), 27(2)(e) read with Section 3(1)(zz)(i), (iii), (iv) and 5 and Section 59 of the Food
Safety and Standards Act, 2006, is seeking anticipatory bail.
2. I have heard the learned counsel for the parties.
3. The record discloses that interim protection was granted to the Applicant on 30.11.2021 and in
pursuance thereof the Sneha Chavan page 1 of 2 20 ABA 2837-21.doc Applicant had attended and
co-operated with the investigating agency.

4. The learned counsel for the Applicant points out that after the receipt of the report of the Analyst
a Statutory Appeal has been filed before the Co-Commissioner of Food and Drugs Administration,
Pune, which is pending.
5. The learned APP states that the Analyst had found that the milk was containing Non Food Grade
Paraffin, which is in breach of the Provisions of Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and Food
Safety and Standards (Food Products Standards and Food Additives) Regulations, 2011. The learned
APP states that the investigation is at a concluding stage and therefore, this Court may pass
appropriate order.
6. In such circumstances, the Criminal Application is disposed of in terms of order dated 30.11.2021
subject to the condition that the Applicant shall co-operate with the investigation and shall not
tamper with the prosecution evidence/witness.
7. The Criminal Application is disposed of in the aforesaid terms.
