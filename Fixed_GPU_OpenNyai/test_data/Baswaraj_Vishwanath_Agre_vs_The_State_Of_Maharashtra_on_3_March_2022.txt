PER COURT :
1. Heard learned Advocate appearing for the applicant.
2. Learned APP strongly opposes the application for grant of any interim relief taking into
consideration the sections under which the offence is registered.
3. Perusal of F.I.R. would show that it has been lodged by Food Safety Officer on 18.08.2021, which
has a reference of the letter by Aurad Shahajani Police Station, Dist. Latur dated 17.08.2021, in
which it was informed that, they had taken banned articles from the possession of the applicant on
15.08.2021. If the police had gone to the spot and seized the articles from the custody of the
applicant, what prevented the police from arresting the applicant is the question, and therefore, this
Court is constrained 902-ABA-1546-2021.odt to grant interim protection to the applicant in order to
seek explanation from the Investigating Officer P.I. S.A. Kamat of Aurad Shahajani Police Station.

4. Issue notice to the respondent, returnable on 17th March, 2022. Learned A.P.P. waives notice for
the respondent - State.
5. In the meantime, in the event of arrest of the applicant in connection with Crime No.154 of 2021
registered with Aurad Shahajani Police Station, District Latur for the offences punishable under
Sections 328, 272, 273 and 188 of Indian Penal Code and Sections 59(2)(i)(ii)(iv), 30(2)(a) and 59 of
the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, he be released on P.R. Bond of Rs. 30,000/- with two
sureties of Rs. 15,000/- each.
6. Applicant shall attend Aurad Shahajani Police Station, Dist. Latur on every Friday between 10.00
a.m. to 2.00 p.m. till further orders and cooperate with the investigation.
7. He shall not tamper with the evidence of the prosecution in any manner.
8. Respondent to file affidavit of P.I. S. A. Kamat, as to why he had not arrested the applicant on
15.08.2021 and also to produce copies of the documents about seizure of the articles by him.
Affidavit to be filed on or before 16.03.2022 by giving a copy of the same to other side in advance.
