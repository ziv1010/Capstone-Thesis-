 ORDER
Learned counsel for the petitioners invited the attention of this Court to Section 89 of the Food
Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and would contend that the Section provides for overriding effect of
this Act over all other food related laws and that the prosecution launched against the petitioners by
the respondents has been initiated under the Prevention of Food Adulteration Act, 1954 which in
presence of Section 89 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006, is not legally valid.
Mr. Amit Gupta, AAG, appearing counsel for the respondents however, would controvert the
contentions of Mr. Pant, learned senior appearing counsel for the petitioners and on contrary would
contend that the prosecution launched against the petitioners under the Prevention of Food
Adulteration Act, 1954 has been initiated validly and legally in view of Section of 97 of the Food
Safety and Standards Act, 2006 which seeks to repeal the enactments and orders specified in second
schedule with effect from the date on which the Act came to be enacted and came into force.
