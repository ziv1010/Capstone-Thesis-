2. The Investigating Officer, while opposing the case of the
applicant for being released on bail before the trial court, pleaded
that the applicant has many small and big pan kiosks and,
therefore, assumed that he must have more contraband articles
with him and this is cited as a reason for seeking his custodial
interrogation. On the basis of the said say of the Investigating
Officer, the Sessions Judge, Kalyan, in the order dated
18/01/2022 branded him as stockist, who had provided the
contraband articles. When learned A.P.P. was asked to provide
the material to that aspect, he was unable to lay his hands upon
any such material.
3. The case of the prosecution is to the effect that, on an
information received on 29/10/2021 to the effect that certain
prohibited substance like tobacco, gutka, pan masala are stored
in a godown located behind Najami General Stores, Mahatma
Phule Chowk, Ulhasnagar, a raid was conducted and, on search,
the prohibited substances like tobacco, scented betel nuts and
allied products were found in huge quantity. This led to the
arrest of one Salim Bhagwan, who owed the said godown and
this led the Investigating Officer to the present applicant.
4. The sketchy connection projected by the main accused in
