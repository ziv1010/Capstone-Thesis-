 ORDER
Order No. 24.01.2022
01. This matter is taken up through video conferencing mode.
Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned counsel for the State.
This is an application under section 438 Cr.P.C. for grant of anticipatory bail to the petitioner in
connection with Malgodown P.S. Case No.259 of 2021 corresponding to G.R. Case No.1600 of 2021
pending in the Court of learned J.M.F.C. (City), Cuttack for alleged commission of offences under
sections 420/273/272/34 of the Indian Penal Code and sections 59 and 63 of Food Safety and
Standards Act, 2000.
In view of available material on record, the // 2 // nature and gravity of the accusation, while not
inclining to grant anticipatory bail to the petitioner, it is observed that in the event the petitioner
surrenders in the Court below and moves for bail before the learned Court below within a period of
four weeks from today, the same shall be disposed of as expeditiously as possible by the Courts
below in accordance with law and claim of parity with the co- accused shall be taken into account at
the time of adjudication of the bail application. The case records shall be made available to the Court
concerned.
