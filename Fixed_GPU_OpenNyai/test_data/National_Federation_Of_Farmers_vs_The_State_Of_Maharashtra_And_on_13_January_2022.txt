The Petition relates to an e-tender issued under a notice dated 18th March 2021 by the State of
Maharashtra for transportation of rice from Food Corporation of India godowns and for the supply
of other food grains, pulses, oil, spices and condiments to schools under the mid-day meal scheme in
13th January 2022 904-ASWP-246-2022.DOC Nashik District. The tender was for one year and was
to cover 3983 schools. The estimated cost was Rs.36.92 crores.
3. The contest today is between the Petitioners and the 3rd Respondent. That Respondent has been
served and given notice. None is present on its behalf today.
4. Mr Kakade confirms that as of today no tender has yet been issued. The statement is noted.
5. Technical bids were opened on 30th September 2021. Commercial or financial bids were opened
on 22nd November 2021. The Petitioner itself says in paragraph 10 that it obtained the documents
submitted by the 3rd Respondent from the online portal. It noticed several discrepancies that
allegedly rendered 3rd Respondent ineligible. While Mr Dhakephalkar raises at least three points of
ineligibility, he emphasizes that one of these is fundamental. That is the requirement that, as on the
date of opening of the technical bids, 30th September 2021, every tenderer must have had a valid
license under the Food Safety and Standard Act ("FSSAI license"). In a pre-bid meeting, in response
to queries raised by bidders, the tendering authority clarified that since the process of obtaining
FSSAI licenses was time-consuming, and since there were lockdowns in various districts, a receipt
or a challan evidencing an application for registration should be submitted and would suffice.
13th January 2022 904-ASWP-246-2022.DOC
6. It appears that the 3rd Respondent did not have a license till as late as 25th November 2021. This
means that certainly on the date of opening of the technical bid on 30th September 2021 (and, in
fact, even at the date of opening of financial bids on 22nd November 2021), the 3rd Respondent did
not hold a valid FSSAI license.
7. Paragraph 10 of the Petition avers that the challan submitted by the 3rd Respondent was dated
16th June 2021 for registration under the FSSAI Act. But the online status of the challan showed
that the 3rd Respondent's application had been rejected. If this is in fact so, then prima facie it
appears to us that the 3rd Respondent could not have been held to be technically qualified.
8. Having regard to these circumstances, we are of the view that Respondents Nos. 1 and
2/tendering authority should not proceed with the award of the tender until a further order of this
Court.
9. Mr Kakade submits that he will take instructions and file a proper Affidavit in response on all
points but including this point in particular.
10. Mr Kakade states that the Affidavit of the tendering authority will be filed and served on or
before 31st January 2022. We will decide a whether a Rejoinder is required on the next date.
