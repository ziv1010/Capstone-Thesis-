 THIS CRIMINAL MISC. CASE HAVING COME UP FOR ADMISSION ON
03.01.2022, THE COURT ON THE SAME DAY PASSED THE FOLLOWING:
 Crl.M.C.No.554 OF 2021 2
 ORDER
Petitioner is the first accused in Crime No. 592/2020 of Ernakulam Central Police Station registered
on 24.05.2020, at the instance of the third respondent where offence under Sections 406, 417, 468,
471 read with 34 of the Indian Penal Code is alleged against the petitioner and the fourth
respondent. The case is under investigation. By the time, the petitioner moved this Court for
quashing the proceedings.
2. I heard the learned counsel for the petitioner, the learned Public Prosecutor and also the
respective counsel for the third and fourth respondents.
3. Now the matter is settled out of the court. In the affidavit dated 29.09.2021, the third respondent
has affirmed that the matter is settled. The fourth respondent also filed an affidavit on 11.11.2021
confirming the settlement. It is purely a personal dispute between the petitioner and the third
respondent which stands settled out of court, so that no public interest is hampered.
4. In the light of the settlement reached between the parties, entire proceedings in Crime No.
592/2020 of Ernakulam Central police station are quashed.
Crl. M.C.is allowed as above.
SD/-
