PER COURT :
1 Heard learned Advocate appearing for the applicant.
2 Learned APP strongly opposes the application for grant of any
interim relief on the ground that the matter is coming for the first time and she is yet to receive the
papers. Contents of the First Information Report shows active involvement of the present applicant.
3 Perusal of the First Information Report would show that Police Constable attached to Gangakhed
Police Station had caught hold of a person, who was going on a motorcycle and he was found
possessing banned articles 2 ABA_317_2022 of Gutkha and Pan Masala. Further, from the remand
report it appears that in all four accused persons arrested thereafter and the last accused i.e. Tavef
Kallu Shaikh made a statement to the police that he had purchased or procured the Gutkha from the
present applicant. At least, at this stage, from the remand report which was taken on 05.11.2021 it is
not appearing that exactly when that statement was made by accused Tavef, but then it appears that
all those five persons were arrested on 04.11.2021. If the name of the present applicant could be
revealed by the police persons around 04.11.2021, they have not conducted any further activity as
