1. The Applicant apprehending arrest in connection with the investigation of Crime No. 54 of 2021 of
Police Station Dadar Sagari, District Raigad under Section 420, 328, 272, 473 of IPC and Section 59
of the Food Safety and Standard Act, 2006, is seeking anticipatory bail.
2. Upon hearing the learned counsel for the parties, it prima facie appears that there is no recovery
of any Gutkha or paan masala from the present Applicant. It appears that the name of the Applicant
was disclosed by accused No.3 Rakesh and accused Sneha Chavan page 1 of 2 9 aba 193-22.doc No.4
Dipen as being the stockist and supplier of Gutkha and paan masala. However, the learned APP in
all fairness pointed out that no such premise belonging to the Applicant are pointed out by the
co-accused and therefore, there is no recovery of any such stock allegedly kept by the present
Applicant.

3. The learned counsel for the Applicant placing reliance on the decision of the learned Single Judge
of this Court sitting at Aurangabad in a group of matters being ABA No. 944/2020 and others
decided on 30.09.2021 has submitted that this Court has taken a view that Section 328 of IPC is not
attracted in such cases.
4. The learned APP has submitted that there is a contrary view taken by another Single Judge of this
Court sitting at Mumbai and all such matters involving the issue pertaining to the scope and ambit
of Section 328 of IPC are kept on 18.02.2022.
5. Hence, stand over to 18.02.2022.
6. Till next date, no coercive steps has been taken against the Applicant, subject to Applicant
reporting to the Investigating Officer on 3rd and 4th February 2022 between 11.00 a.m. to 1.00 p.m.
and co-operating with the investigating agency.
