3. According to the informant, from 10 January 2020 to 11

manufacturing of artificial milk were recovered.
 (i) Whole Milk Powder 956 Packets of 500 gms each.
 (ii) Whey Permeate Powder 50 Bags of 25 Kgs each.
 (iii) Lactose Powder 33 Bags
 (iv) Liquid Paraffin 1 Barrel of 1258 Litres.
 (v) Sorbitol 1 Barrel of 1298 Litres.
