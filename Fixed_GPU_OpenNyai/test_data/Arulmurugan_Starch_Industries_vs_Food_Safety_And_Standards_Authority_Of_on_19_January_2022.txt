 ORDER
This Writ Petition has been filed by the petitioner, praying for issuance of a Writ of Certiorarified
Mandamus, to call for the entire records relating to the notice in Roc.No.1350/A1/FSSA/2017 dated
06.07.2018 and consequential order of suspension dated 12.07.2018 on the file of the 3rd
respondent herein and quash the same and consequently direct the 2nd respondent herein to
consider the representation dated 11.07.2018 of the petitioner herein seeking an enquiry into the
abuse of the power by the 3rd respondent herein for extraneous consideration to favour the Large
Scale Sago manufacturers under the guise of ensuring Food Safety and Standards prescribed under
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
2. When this Writ Petition is taken up for hearing, the learned counsel for the petitioner would
submit that the petitioner also preferred an appeal before the 2nd respondent-Commissioner of
Food Safety as against the impugned order in the Writ Petition. However, as the said appeal has not
been disposed of so far, the petitioner has approached the Court.
3. Mrs.C.Meera Arumugam, the learned Additional Government Pleader for respondents 2 and 3
takes notice and submitted that if any direction is given by this Court, the same will be complied
with.
4. Therefore, taking into consideration that the appeal preferred by the petitioner is still pending
