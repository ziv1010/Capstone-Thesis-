This Criminal Original Petition has been filed by the petitioner/A3 to quash the complaint in
CC.No.131 of 2017 pending on the file of the Judicial Magistrate, Ambattur, Chennai.
2. It is the case of the complainant that one Manna Health Mix, Southern Health Foods Pvt Ltd., is
the manufacturer of the alleged health drinks and samples were lifted and the same were sent for
the analysis and report was received on 10.06.2014. As per the report, the sample were found to be
sub-standard, unsafe and misbranded. Thereby, the respondent/complainant had filed the private
complaint against the petitioner and other accused for the offence under Sections 3(1)(zx),
3(1)(zz)(v)(x) and 3(1)(zf)(a-i) of Food Safety and Standardization Act, 2006. The subsequent
samples also reveal that the product was sub-standard and unsafe.
3. Heard Mr.R.Vijayaragavan, the learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr.A.Gokulakrishnan,
learned Additional Public Prosecutor appearing for the respondent.
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that admittedly, as per the complaint, the health mix
which was subjected to test was produced by Southern Health Food Private Limited having office at
AL-84, 1st Floor, 4th Street, II Main Road, Anna Nagar, Chennai - 40 and the petitioner is the
nominee of the company and as per the Section 66 of FSS Act, a mandate is cast on the complainant
to proceed against the company which has produced whereas in this case, the company was not
impleded. The private complaint which has been filed without impleding the company is bad in law,
thereby he prayed for quashing the proceedings. Further, the learned counsel would submit that in
respect of a co-accused in the very same case, this Court in Crl.OP.No.19747 of 2017 dated
26.11.2021 taking into consideration that the company was not impleaded as accused, has quashed
the proceedings.
5. The learned Additional Public Prosecutor submitted that as per the Section 66 of the Act, when
the offences are committed by the company, the company has to be added as an accused. However,
he would fairly concede that the company has not been added as accused in this case.
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis
6. Perusal of records including the complaint reveals that the health mix had been produced by
M/s.Southern Health Food Private Limited. However, in the complaint, the company has not been
added as an accused and there is no averments against the present petitioner that he is in-charge
and responsible for the conduct of the business of the company. Accordingly, the entire prosecution
launched against the petitioner cannot be sustained in the eye of law. Further, the continuation of
the proceedings as against the petitioner is an abuse of process of law. Hence, the proceedings has to
be necessarily quashed against the petitioner.
7. Accordingly, this Criminal Original Petition is allowed, the proceedings pending in C.C.No.131 of
2017on the file of the learned Judicial Magistrate Ambattur, Chennai in respect of the petitioner is
hereby quashed. Consequently, connected miscellaneous petitions are closed.
