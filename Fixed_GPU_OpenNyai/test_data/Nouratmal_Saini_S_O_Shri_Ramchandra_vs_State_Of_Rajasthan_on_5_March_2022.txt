HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE NARENDRA SINGH DHADDHA Order 05/03/2022 In view of the fact
that a criminal prosecution has been launched against the petitioner under the penal provisions of
the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and for the identical nature of allegations, the impugned
FIR has been lodged, wherein, after investigation charge-sheet has been submitted and the learned
Trial Court has taken cognizance of the offence and proceeded further in the matter, I feel persuaded
to interfere in the matter.
Issue notice.
Learned Public Prosecutor accepts notice on behalf of the State.
Issue notice to the respondent No.2 only. Learned Public Prosecutor is directed to file a response to
the petition as to why the petition may not be allowed in toto.
List after six seeks.
In the meanwhile, further proceedings arising out of FIR No.317/2019 registered at Police Station
Phulera, District Jaipur Rural including the criminal proceedings bearing criminal (2 of 2)
[CRLMP-2336/2022] regular case No.508/2021 in the Court of Judicial Magistrate, Samberlake,
shall remain stayed.
