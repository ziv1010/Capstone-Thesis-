 THE HON'BLE SRI JUSTICE D. RAMESH
 CRIMINAL PETITION NO.1598 OF 2022
ORDER:
-
This Criminal Petition under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (for short
"Cr.P.C."), is filed seeking quash of the proceedings in F.I.R.No.184 of 2020 of Tadikonda Police
Station, Guntur (Urban) Guntur District, registered for the offences punishable under Sections
57(1), 58, 59, 63 of Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and under Section 34(A) of Andhra
Pradesh Excise Act, 1968.
2. Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and the learned Additional Public Prosecutor for the
State.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioners would submit that this is a covered matter in view of the
earlier common order passed by this Court in Criminal Petition Nos.5421 of 2019 and batch,
whereby this Court has quashed several F.I.Rs registered for the similar offences based on identical
facts. Therefore, he would submit that the petitioners are similarly placed and they are also entitled
for quash of the F.I.R registered against them, leaving the offence punishable under Section 34(A) of
A.P. Excise Act, to be investigated.
4. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor fairly concedes that this is a covered matter in view of the
aforesaid common order passed by this Court and thereby prayed to pass appropriate orders
accordingly in this Criminal Petition also.
5. Therefore, in view of the aforesaid common order of this Court and for the reasons stated therein,
this Criminal Petition is partly allowed and the aforesaid F.I.R. registered against the petitioners, is
hereby quashed, with regard to the offences punishable under Sections 57(1), 58, 59, 63 of Food
Safety and Standards Act, 2006. However, the Criminal Petition relating to the offence punishable
under Section 34(A) of the A.P.Excise Act, is hereby dismissed. The Investigating Officer is at liberty
to proceed with investigation relating to the offence punishable under Section 34(A) of the
A.P.Excise Act.
Miscellaneous Petitions, if any pending, in this Criminal Petition, shall stand closed.
