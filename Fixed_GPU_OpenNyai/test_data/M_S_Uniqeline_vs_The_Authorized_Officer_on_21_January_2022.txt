 ORDER
The petitioner has filed this writ petition seeking issuance of Writ of Mandamus directing the
respondent to lift the alert/ comments made in the online portal of FSSAI dated 09.04.2021, as
against the petitioner proprietorship concern, M/s.Uniqeline (FSSAI ID No:ANUPP4153ECH001)
and to consequently to allow/ permit the petitioner to operate through the online portal of
respondent/ FSSAI by also considering the representation of the petitioner dated 19.04.2021.
2.The case of the petitioner is that the petitioner concern is a proprietorship concern, originally
issued with Custom House Agent (in short CHA) CHA License by the Commissioner of Customs,
Chennai in the year 2010 and valid upto 2030 with one A.Prabhakaran being the Authorized
Signatory and Proprietor of CHA concern and has been authorized to transact business as Custom
House Agent.
3.The further case of the petitioner is that the respondent https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis without
proving any fault or malafide as against the petitioner uploaded the comment 'Kindly note that no
application submitted by the CHA M/s.Uniqeline for FSSAI clearance will be processed till the
investigation on the matter regarding the customs clearance taken for the FSSAI Rejected product
pertaining to Bill of Entry no.7930036 dated 17.06.2020 filed by the CHA on behalf of importer
M/s.Amphora Commodities Pvt. Limited is completed' in their official website, thereby restraining
the petitioner from further acting as CHA and the same had totally crippled the business activities of
the petitioner. In this regard, the petitioner made representation to the Chairman, FSSAI and other
Higher Authorities with copy marked to the respondent and since the same evoked no response, has
filed this writ petition.
4.The learned counsel appearing for the petitioner submitted that during the pendancy of this writ
petition, the said portal comment was removed and during December, 2021, the petitioner made
five applications and all the five applications were already forwarded and subsequently the
petitioner was allowed to operate the bill of entry on behalf of the importers and hence, nothing
survives https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis for adjudication in this writ petition.
