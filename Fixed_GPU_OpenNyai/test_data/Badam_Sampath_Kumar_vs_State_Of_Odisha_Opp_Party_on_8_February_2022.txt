 ORDER
Order No. 08.02.2022
01. This matter is taken up through Hybrid arrangement (video conferencing/physical mode).
Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned counsel for the State.
This is an application under section 438 Cr.P.C. for grant of anticipatory bail to the petitioner in
connection with Semiliguda P.S. Case No.108 of 2021 corresponding to G.R. Case No.71 of 2021
pending in the Court of learned J.M.F.C., Semiliguda for alleged commission of offences under
sections 420/272/273/34 of the Indian Penal Code and sections 51/52/56/57/59 of Food Safety and
Standards Act, 2006.
// 2 // In view of available material on record and the accusation against the petitioner that the
petitioner was manufacturing Vimal Brand Pan Masala by using different expired chemicals which
were injurious for human consumption, while not inclining to grant anticipatory bail to the
petitioner, it is observed that in the event the petitioner surrenders in the Court below and moves
for bail before the learned Court below within a period of four weeks from today, the same shall be
disposed of as expeditiously as possible by the Courts below in accordance with law. The case
records shall be made available to the Court concerned.
