 Order
27/01/2022
 It has been submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners
that a complaint (Annexure-10) has been filed in the learned trial Court under Section 16 of the
Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006.
It is further submitted that second RFL report was received on 16.06.2014, whereas the life of the
product expired before the date of report. The manufacturing date of product was January, 2013 and
it was also mentioned that it was best before nine months from the date of manufacture.
It is also submitted that in a similar matter, co-ordinate Bench of this Court in Criminal Misc.
Petition No.5486/2021 has passed the order dated 09.12.2021 staying the further proceedings
pending before the trial court.
Learned Public Prosecutor has opposed the application (I.A. No.1/2022).
