 ORDER
This Writ Petition has been filed to issue a Writ of Mandamus, directing the 2nd respondent herein
to consider the Appeal dated 18.06.2018 with Petition for Stay filed by the petitioner herein under
Section 32 (4) (a) of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 challenging the improvement Notice
issued by the 3rd respondent herein in his proceedings in Na.Ka.No.826/A1/FSS ACT/2014-18
dated 04.05.2018 on merits and pass orders in accordance with law and direct the respondents 3
and 4 herein to https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis ensure impartiality and transparency in
implementing the Food Safety and Standards Act and consciously refrain from doing any act which
would facilitate monopoly in Sago manufacturing industry and give room for manipulation of Sago
market in favour of a few individuals.
2. When the matter is taken up for hearing, the learned counsel appearing for the petitioner submits
that the said Appeal dated 18.06.2018 was disposed of. However, he submits that the 3rd and 4th
respondents are acting impartiality against the petitioner / Sri Balamurugan Sago Industries.
Therefore, the learned counsel for the petitioner seeks appropriate instructions for the respondents
to ensure impartiality and transparency in implementing the Food Safety and Standards Act.
3. On the other hand, the learned Special Government Pleader appearing for the respondents would
submit that all the officers are acting impartiality and there is no truth in the submissions made by
the petitioner. Further he submits that the Appeal was also disposed of.
