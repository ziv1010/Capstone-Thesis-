 ORDER
https://www.mhc.tn.gov.in/judis and The challenge in this Writ Petition is to the order passed by
the second respondent, in Case No. 48/2017/M2, dated 11.05.2018.
2. When the matter is taken up for hearing today, the learned counsel for the petitioner submitted
that the impugned order was passed by the second respondent against the petitioner for offence
punishable under Section 57 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. The learned counsel
contended that, challenging the order passed by the second respondent, though there was an Appeal
provision before the Food Safety Appellate Tribunal, the same was not notified when the petitioner
filed this Writ Petition in the year 2018. However, subsequently, the Food Safety Appellate Tribunal
has been notified and it started to function from the year 2020 onwards. Hence, the learned counsel
prayed to dispose of this Writ Petition granting liberty to the petitioner to approach the Food Safety
Appellate Tribunal.

3. The learned counsel appearing for the respondents submits that, as stated by the learned counsel
for the petitioner, since the Food Safety Appellate Tribunal is functioning, the issue involved in this
Writ Petition can very well be adjudicated before the said Appellate Tribunal.
4. In view of the above submissions made by the learned counsel on either side, this Writ Petition
stands disposed of, by granting liberty to the petitioner to challenge the impugned order by way of
filing Appeal Petition before the Food Safety Appellate Tribunal within a period of two weeks from
the date of receipt of a copy of this order. In such an event, the aforesaid Tribunal shall entertain the
same, without rejecting it on the ground of limitation. Consequently, connected miscellaneous
petition is closed.
