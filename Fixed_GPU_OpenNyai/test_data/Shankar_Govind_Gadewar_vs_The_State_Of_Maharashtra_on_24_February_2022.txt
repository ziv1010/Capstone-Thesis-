1. Heard learned Advocate Mr. B. N. Gadegaonkar for the applicant.
2. Learned APP has strong objection for grant of interim relief as the matter is
coming for the first time and he is yet to receive the papers. He also submits that the
huge banned articles were found with co-accused, and taking into consideration the
papers for which the articles have been banned, sympathy need not be shown to the
applicant.
3. Perusal of the FIR would show that the name of the present applicant has not been
reflected and it appears that the name of the present applicant has been disclosed by
co-accused. It is then required to be seen as to what role has been attributed by the
co-
2 ABA 208-2022 accused to the present applicant, but then till the papers are perused, the liberty of
the applicant need not be curtailed.
4. Issue notice to the respondent-State, stand over to 14-03- 2022.
5. Learned APP waives notice for respondent-State.
