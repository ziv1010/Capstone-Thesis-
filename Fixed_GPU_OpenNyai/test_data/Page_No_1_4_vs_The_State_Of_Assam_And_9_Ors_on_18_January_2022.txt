BEFORE HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE MANASH RANJAN PATHAK 18.01.2022 Heard Mr. C. Sharma,
learned counsel for the petitioners and Mr. B. Gogoi, learned Standing Counsel, Health and Family
Welfare Department, Assam for the respondent Nos. 1, 4, 6, 8 and 9. Also heard Mr. N. Goswami,
learned Government Advocate, Assam for the respondent Nos. 2, 5, 7 & 10 and Ms. A. Gayan,
learned CGC for the respondent No. 3.
By this application, the petitioners have prayed for implementation of the Notification No.
FSC.12/2013/11 dated 12.01.2021 issued by the State Government in the Food and Drugs
Administration, Assam, Commissionerate of Food Safety, Bamunimaidan, Guwahati whereby the
State Government prohibited articles of food in which tobacco and/or nicotine are used as
ingredients being injuries to human health in terms of Regulation 2.3.4 of the Food Safety and
Standards (Prohibition and Restrictions of Sales) Regulations, 2011 read with Section 26 of Food
Safety and Standards Act, 2006. The said Notification dated 12.01.2021 was issued with due
approval of the Government in the Health and Family Welfare Department.
By the said Notification dated 12.01.2021, the State Government prohibited the Page No.# 4/4
manufacture, storage, transportation, display, distribution or sale of Gutkha, Pan Masala or any
chewing material etc. by whatsoever name, containing tobacco or nicotine as ingredients available in
the market for a period of 1 (one) year under Clause (a) of sub-section 2 of Section 30 of Food Safety
and Standards Act, 2006 in the interest of public health with immediate effect.
The said Notification dated 12.01.2021 was issued by the State Government in exercise of power of
Regulation 2.3.4 of said 2011 Regulations and Section 26 of said 2006 Act.
Since the State Government did not implement the said Notification dated 12.01.2021, the
petitioners have preferred this writ petition.
From the perusal of the impugned Notification dated 12.01.2021 it is seen that it was only for a
period of 1 (one) year from the date of its issuance, i.e. upto 11.01.2022 and as such the said
Notification dated 12.01.2021 spent its force.
