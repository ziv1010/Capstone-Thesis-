PER COURT :
1 Heard learned Advocate appearing for the applicant.
2 Learned APP strongly opposes the application for grant of any
interim relief, on the ground that the matter is coming for the first time and he is yet to receive the
papers. Contents of the First Information Report shows active involvement of the present applicant.
3 Perusal of the First Information Report would show that the applicant was not present at the spot
with contraband articles, but it has been stated that those articles containing Gutkha which is
banned in Maharashtra 2 ABA_252_2022 was caught by Jalgaon Police by Special Unit on
Nizar-Nandurbar road. Further, it appears that during the course of the investigation it was found
out that those articles were stored in the godown of the present applicant within the jurisdiction of
Gujrat State. Learned Advocate for the applicant submits that Gutkha is not a banned article in
Gujrat State. Therefore, till the papers are perused, as regards connection to be established, prima
facie, he deserves to be protected.
