2. Mr. Alag submits that the case of the appellant in the said writ petition was that on 07.08.2020,
the Conciliation Officer had passed an 'order' to the effect that retrenchment of 66 workmen was
undertaken without compliance of Section 25N(7) and that the appellant workmen are deemed to be
in the employment of the respondent/ Delhi Golf Club in the absence of permission from the
Appropriate Government. Mr. Alag submits that it was in this light that W.P.(C.) No.5440/2020 was
filed to seek the reliefs, as set out in the aforesaid extract.
3. The said position taken by the appellant was seriously contested by the respondents, who claimed
that the file noting dated 07.08.2020 made by the Conciliation Officer was not an 'order' and the
Conciliation Officer had no authority, or jurisdiction to pass any order. Several other pleas were also
raised by the respondent. The Labour Department had also filed an affidavit stating that the

4. A perusal of the impugned judgment shows that the aforesaid aspect has not received

5. The appeal stands disposed of in the aforesaid terms.
