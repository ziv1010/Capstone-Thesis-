 THE HONOURABLE SRI JUSTICE D.RAMESH
 CRIMINAL PETITION No.6085 of 2019
O R D E R:
This Criminal Petition is filed under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 seeking to
quash the proceedings against the petitioners/A1 to A3 in P.R.C.No.53 of 2019 on the file of the
Junior Civil Judge cum First Additional Judicial Magistrate of First Class, Badvel for the offences
punishable under sections 9(1) of A.P. Gaming Act (Cricket betting), Sections188, 272, 273, 328 of
IPC and Sections 57, 58 read with 26, 30 of Food Safety Standards Act-2006 [for short FSS Act].
2. Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and learned Assistant Public Prosecutor appearing for
the State.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioners while denying the allegations made in the charge sheet,
primarily assails the validity of the criminal prosecution launched against the petitioners under
Section 188 IPC, on the ground that the bar engrafted under Section 195 Cr.P.C. clearly attracts to
the present facts of the case, as there is no such complaint filed by the concerned authority, who had
issued the initial order, said to be violated, or a person, who is administratively superior to the
officer who issued the order. He further submits that even the allegations made in the FIR and
charge sheet does not bring out the commission of any other offences alleged under sections 9(1) of
A.P. Gaming Act, Section 272, 273, 328 of IPC and Section 57, 58 read with 26, 30 of FSS Act, 2006,
since the tobacco products would not fall within the definition of Section 3(j) of FSS Act. In the
similar circumstances, earlier this court in Criminal Petition Nos.5146 of 2019 and 3731 of 2018 and
batch quashed the F.I.Rs registered for the similar offences punishable under Indian Penal Code
and FSS Act.
4. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor fairly concedes that except the offences under Gaming Act,
other offences were covered in the earlier orders of this Court, thereby prayed to pass appropriate
order in this Criminal Petition.
5. Therefore, in view of the fact that this matter is squarely covered by the earlier orders passed by
this Court and in view of the legal position discussed therein, this Criminal Petition is partly
allowed, quashing the proceedings against the petitioners in P.R.C.No.53 of 2019 on the file of the
Junior Civil Judge cum First Additional Judicial Magistrate of First Class, Badvel for the offences
punishable under Section188, 272, 273, 328 of IPC and Sections 57, 58 read with 26, 30 of FSS Act.
