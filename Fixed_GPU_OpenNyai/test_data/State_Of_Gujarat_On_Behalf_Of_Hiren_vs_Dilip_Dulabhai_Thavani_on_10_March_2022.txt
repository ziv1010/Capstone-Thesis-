 ORAL ORDER
Order in the Criminal Misc. Application:-
This application is filed by the appellant-State for leave to appeal against the
judgment of acquittal dated 23/03/2021 passed by the learned Judicial Magistrate
First Class, Junagadh in Criminal Case No.392 of 2013 whereby learned Magistrate
was pleased to acquit the respondent accused for the offence punishable under
Sections 3(1)(ZZ)(IV)(VII)(VIII) and Section 26(2)(I)(II), 59(I) of Food Safety and
Standard Act, 2006.
Heard Mr. H. K. Patel, learned APP for the appellant- State.
Nobody appeared in present application for the respondent.
