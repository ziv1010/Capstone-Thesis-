HARNARESH SINGH GILL, J. (ORAL)
Case is taken up for hearing through video conferencing. Through the instant petition under Section
438 Cr.P.C., the petitioner seeks anticipatory bail in case bearing FIR No. 101 dated 28.07.2021,
registered under Sections 272, 273, 420 IPC and Section 59 of the Food Safety and Standards Act,
2006, at Police Station Balongi, District SAS Nagar.
Vide order dated 22.10.2021 passed by a Coordinate Bench, ad interim anticipatory bail was granted
to the petitioner.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that pursuant to the aforesaid order dated 22.10.2021,
the petitioner has joined the investigation.
Learned State counsel, on instructions from ASI Surender Singh, submits that the petitioner has
joined the investigation and he is not required for any further investigation.

absolute, subject to the conditions laid down in Section 438 (2) of the Code of Criminal Procedure.
