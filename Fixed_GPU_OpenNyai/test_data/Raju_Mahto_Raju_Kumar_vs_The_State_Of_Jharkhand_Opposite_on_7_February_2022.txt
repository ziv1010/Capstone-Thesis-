Order No.02 Dated- 07.02.2022 Heard the parties through video conferencing. Learned counsel for
the petitioner personally undertakes to remove the defects as pointed out by the stamp reporter
within two weeks after resumption of the Court in physical mode. In view of the personal
undertaking of the learned counsel for the petitioner, the defects pointed out by the stamp reporter
are ignored for the present.
Apprehending his arrest, the petitioner has moved this Court for grant of privilege of anticipatory
bail in connection with Bagodar P.S. Case No.172 of 2021 registered under sections 272/273/34 of
the Indian Penal Code and under Section 48/59 of Food Safety and Standard Act, 2006.
The Learned Spl. P.P. submits that the offences involved in this case are all bailable in nature as the
offence punishable under Section 59 (i) of Food Safety and Standard Act, 2006 is only made out
against the petitioner from the allegations made against him, for which maximum sentence
prescribed is six months. Hence, it is submitted that this anticipatory bail application is not
maintainable.
This contention of the learned Special P.P. is not contested by the learned counsel for the petitioner.
Considering the facts of the case and the submission of learned Spl. P.P., this anticipatory bail
application of the petitioner is dismissed being not maintainable as all the offences alleged in this
case are bailable in nature hence, the petitioner has failed to make out the case that there is any
