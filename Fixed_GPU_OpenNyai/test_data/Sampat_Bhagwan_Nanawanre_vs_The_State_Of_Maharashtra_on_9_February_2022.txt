3. The allegation as per FIR is that there was adulteration of the
milk by addition of Nirma Powder and other detergents. In so far as
the present Applicant is concerned, he is only the driver on the
vehicle in which the milk was being transported. It appears that
there was a transport contract between the employer of the present
Applicant and the company for transport of the milk.
4. Learned Counsel for the Applicant submitted that the
Applicant is not even an employee of the main Accused who are
alleged to have committed adulteration of the milk.
5. Learned APP, in all fairness, did not dispute that the only role
of the Applicant is that he was a driver on the vehicle and in spite of
knowledge, he did not report the matter of adulteration to the
authorities. In other words, there is no allegation that the Applicant
was actively involved in the adulteration of the milk. The record
discloses that there is interim protection operating in favour of the
Applicant since 29 October 2021.
6. In such circumstances, the following order is passed.
 ORDER
(i) In the event of his arrest in connection with investigation of Crime No.557/2021 of Police Station
Baramati, Pune Rural, the Applicant be released on Mamta Kale page 2 of 3 20-aba-2622-2021 bail
on executing a P.R. Bond in the sum of Rs.25,000/- with one or two solvent sureties in the like
amount.
(ii) The Applicant shall report to the Investigating Officer on 17/2/2022 and 18/2/2022 between
11.00 a.m. to 1.00 p.m. and as and when directed and shall cooperate with the Investigating Agency.
(iii) The Applicant shall not assist himself in any manner with the adulteration, transport and/or
distribution of the adulterated milk.
(iv) In the event of breach of any of the conditions, the bail is liable to be cancelled.
(v) The Criminal Application is disposed of, in the aforesaid terms.
