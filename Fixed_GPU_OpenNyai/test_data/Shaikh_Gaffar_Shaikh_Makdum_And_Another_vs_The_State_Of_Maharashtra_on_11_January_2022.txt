PER COURT :
1 Heard learned Advocate appearing for the applicants. Though
the learned APP is strongly opposing the application, the remand report dated 05.11.2021 reveals
that on the statement of co-accused it is stated that names of these applicants are revealed, however,
the said report is silent about exact role of the applicants alleged by the prosecution. Under such
circumstance, till the papers are perused the applicants deserve to be protected.
2 Issue notice to respondent, returnable on 25.01.2022. Learned 2 ABA_1465_2021 APP waives
notice for the respondent.
3 In the meantime, in the event of arrest of the applicants viz. Shaikh Gaffar s/o Shaikh Makdum
and Ferozkhan s/o Wahabkhan Pathan, in connection with Crime No.430 of 2021 dated 03.11.2021,
registered with Gangakhed Police Station, Dist. Parbhani, for the offence punishable under Section
188, 328, 272, 273 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 and under Section 59 of the Food, Safety and
Standards Act, 2006, they be released on P.R. of Rs.30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand only) with
