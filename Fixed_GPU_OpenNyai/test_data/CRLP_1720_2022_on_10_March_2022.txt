 CRIMINAL PETITION NO.1720 OF 2022
ORDER:
-
This Criminal Petition under Section 482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 ( for short
"Cr.P.C."), is filed seeking quash of the proceedings in C.C.No.69 of 2020 on the file of the
Additional Junior Civil Judge, Mangalagiri registered for the offences punishable under Sections
188, 272, 273, 420 IPC and Sections 58, 59 & 63 of Food Safety and Standard Act, 2006.
2. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and the learned Additional Public Prosecutor for the
State.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that this is a covered matter in view of the earlier
common order passed by this Court in Criminal Petition Nos.3731 of 2018 and batch, whereby this
Court has quashed several Charge Sheets as well as F.I.Rs filed for the similar offences based on
identical facts. Therefore, he would submit that the petitioner is similarly placed and therefore,
entitled for quash of the Charge Sheet filed against him.
4. Learned Additional Public Prosecutor fairly concedes that this is a covered matter in view of the
aforesaid common order passed by this Court and thereby prayed to pass appropriate orders
accordingly in this Criminal Petition also.
5. Therefore, in view of the aforesaid common order of this Court and for the reasons stated therein,
this Criminal Petition is allowed and the aforesaid Charge Sheet filed against the petitioner, is
hereby quashed.
Miscellaneous Petitions, if any pending, in this Criminal Petition, shall stand closed.
